# Snow and extreme-weather days at ten European airports, 2025: synthesis

Sources: the ten per-airport accounts (EHAM, LFPG, EDDF, EDDM, LSZH, EGLL, LTFM, LIRF, LEMD, LEBL) and their tables
(`<APT>_days.csv`, the class, hourly, pier, runway, band, intensity and analogue tables, `de_gap_by_turnaround.csv`);
the accounts and their tables stay in the development repository and are not published. Training data: all 2025
departures with targets; ranking months: January and July 2026 without targets.

Conventions. "Matched" means the same weekday on normal days near the event (no event, recovery or holiday); "excess" is the event day minus its pooled matched days. "Proxy" is NM's taxi-out reading `takeoff - AOBT_3`; "gap" is `target - proxy` = `AOBT_3(NM) - block time (airport)`; "gap shift" is the gap's excess over matched days, so a positive shift means the target grew more than NM's reading did (the proxy under-reads the event) and a negative shift means NM's reading grew more (the proxy over-reads). Hours are local. Base-model figures are out-of-fold: January and July from `v44_direct_blend` (70 % a stack that carries the ADS-B stage, so it sees part of the actual taxi and is tighter than the feature model alone), February and December from `lean2_window_winter` (the winter split). **March, October and November 2025 are in neither OOF**, which removes the closest 2026 twins (EHAM 20/23/25 Nov, LFPG 21-23 Nov, LSZH 21 Nov, EDDF 22-23 Nov, EGLL 19 Nov) from every model table below.

Comparability caveat. The five analyst teams matched days with slightly different windows (±6 weeks at EHAM/LFPG, ±45 d at EDDF/EDDM, ±42 d at LSZH/EGLL, calendar month ±1 at LTFM/LIRF and LEMD/LEBL) and flagged events with their own thresholds, so a cross-airport excess is comparable to about ±30-50 s; the day-to-day noise of a matched-day mean is 10-30 s at LEMD, 30-45 s at LEBL and similar elsewhere. Excesses under ~40 s are not findings anywhere.

Instead of cancelling, the airports pushed back late: median pushback 2,416 s behind schedule on EHAM 5 Jan and 1,677 s on 18 Jan (575 s normally), 3,418 s on LFPG 22 Nov, with 56-75 % of departures more than 15 min late; EGLL off-block delay 380-825 s against 30-130 on matched days. Where thinning did occur, the surviving flights were the slowest of the year (EDDF 5 Jan 05-09 at 25 % of departures with +1,086 s; EDDM 5 Jan at 26 % with +1,840 s; LTFM 22 Feb at 66 % with a 36-minute mean), so a low hourly count with a high proxy anomaly is a disruption signature, not a quiet hour. A consequence seen at every airport: the "stale-looking" share (target = takeoff - schedule within 2 min) **falls** on event days (EHAM 2-6 % vs 15-20 %, LFPG 4 % vs 16-19 %, EDDM 5-15 % vs 22-25 %, LSZH 6-17 % vs 10-22 %, EGLL 6-15 % vs 17-20 %, LTFM 6-18 % vs 20 %, LEMD/LEBL -4 to -14 points): at nine airports that fingerprint marks punctual pushbacks. Only at LIRF does the genuine stale defect rise with disruption (1.3 % to 7.1 % of departures on 13 Jul; 14 of 15 targets above two hours that day are schedule copies).

### 2.2 The hourly course: three shapes plus convection

1. **Morning de-icing wave that fades by early afternoon** (snow mornings everywhere, frost mornings, most freezing-fog days outside Paris). Pooled snow classes: EHAM +180..+400 s per departure at 05-09 (peak 08: 1,122 vs 724 s), +90..+150 at 10-11, within ±60 from 12; LFPG snow +300..+840 at 05-09 (peak 08: 1,786 vs 948, 50 % above 30 min), under +100 from 16; EDDM snow +590..+890 every hour 06-13; EGLL snow +340 at 08-09, flat afternoon; LEMD dry frost +279 / +293 / +359 at 06 / 07 / 08, +100 at 09, zero from 10; LSZH 06-10 +100..+500. Single days: EHAM 5 Jan 06-12 at 1,640-2,820 s mean, 13 local +52, evening 250-350 s *below* matched; LFPG 23 Nov 07-08 +1,430 / +1,700, normal from 16; EDDF 5 Jan peak 09 (+1,698) then +622 / +265 / +100 by block; EDDM 5 Jan +2,846 at 08, gone by 16.
2. **All-day with an evening re-build** when the weather persists or returns: LFPG freezing fog +420..+940 at 04-10, +760 / +850 at 11-12, trough +100..+170 at 15-19, +250..+640 at 20-23 (17 Jan above 2,300 s from 07 to 12 and 2,000-2,340 at 21-23); LSZH two waves (06-10 and 18-23) with the last hour the worst (+360..+400, 18-20 % above 30 min) because the 23:30 curfew compresses the late queue; EDDM 24 Dec building to +2,222 at 14 local and still +800 at 19, 16 Feb to +1,565 at 19, 21 Jan fog to +907 at 23; EGLL 31 Dec evening +575..+1,179 from 20 h; EHAM only when the weather arrived late (20 Nov evening snow peak 23 local +1,367; 28 Dec +485 in the evening).
3. **Tracking precipitation intensity with a one- to two-hour lag, no bank structure** (LTFM): 19 Feb snow began 07:50 local, +160 at 08, +2,181 at 09, +4,000 at 10-11, lull 12-14, a second build to +3,900 at 17-18 as the heaviest snow fell (1.6 cm/h at 17), +800 at 23; pooled over the ten snow days the night bank 03-06 (+1,300..+1,460), the late-morning wave 09-11 (+900..+1,640) and 22-23 (+820..+1,190) are the peaks and 14-16 (+310..+570) the trough.
4. **Fog follows the LVP hours exactly**: EGLL 31 Dec +1,288 / +1,151 at 10-11 h during FZFG 09:50-11:20Z, +375 at 12, zero by 14; 1 Mar +610..+685 at 07-09 (the fog hours); EDDF 17 Dec +450..+743 at 08-13 under LVP to 14Z, gone by 16; EDDM freezing fog +560..+750 at 07-10, +80..+250 after 15.
5. **Convection is an afternoon-and-evening event**: LIRF +335..+865 from 10 to 17 local and decaying after 18 (13 Jul: +2,346 at 12 and +850..+1,550 until midnight, ten hours after the storm); LEMD nothing before 15, +80..+135 per hour 16-22; LEBL peaks at 14 and 19-20 (+148..+163) with a morning contribution when cells arrive early; LFPG 21 Jul +199 in the afternoon/evening. Morning storms barely register (LIRF 10 Jan 2026 proxy +9; LEMD 2 May 2025 is the exception with +573 in the 06-10 block).

Rule of thumb across the first four shapes: **the uplift peaks one to two hours after the onset of snow or freezing fog and is back to normal two to four hours after the last report**, unless the next bank arrives first.

Who gets de-iced is structured (EDDM, 15 event days, `de_gap_by_turnaround.csv`): gap +243 s for turnarounds under 90 min (12 % above 900 s), +411 for 1.5-4 h, +510 for 4-10 h, **+571 for aircraft parked overnight (29 % above 900 s)**; heavies +578 vs mediums +330 (A380-class +1,200); hub carrier +364, two low-cost operators +168..+190, two long-haul operators +700..+750. Within a day the gap of consecutive departures is only weakly persistent (0.30: slots are drawn per flight), whereas on EDDF's runway-closure days it is a system state (0.56 within the pier, uniform over turnaround length). At EGLL the de-icing is an operator effect: the T5B / T5C satellites take 2-4x the uplift of T5A and 5-20x T2 / T4 on every winter event (31 Dec T5B +1,070 vs T2 +51; 11 Jan T5C +995 vs T4 +34) with the pier shares unchanged.

### 2.5 Next-day carry-over

Nil once the weather has gone: EHAM +3 s over 40 recovery days (the day after each of the five strongest events -153..+60), LFPG +4 over 36 (23 Nov -> 24 Nov -65), LSZH +11 on the unflagged day after a snow day (morning -9), LEMD after frost or convection (2 May -> -67), LEBL after convection (-4..-16). Present when the cold or the cover persists: EDDM +190..+300 on five of six days after a big day (all with Tmin below about -2 C; the unflagged Christmas Day +235 on 1 cm of depth), EDDF 21 Jan +149 / 23 Nov +258 (both cold days themselves; otherwise within +70), LTFM 24 Feb **+642** on 8 cm of cover at -6.9 C with zero snowfall (03-11 local +1,150..+2,800, normal from 13; 25 Feb -47). Present through rolling backlog at the one capacity-saturated airport: EGLL +99 on the unflagged day after a fog day (18 Dec +243, 3 Oct +136), +65 after snow, the frost pair 10-11 Jan rolling into 12 Jan (+132). Multi-day rain carries at LEMD (7 -> 8 -> 9 Mar +123 / +152 / +156) and LEBL (16 -> 17 Oct +140). Rome's 14 Jul (+570) is 47 % stale rows and one wrong-date row.

Three mechanisms behind the compression. (a) At EDDM the model has learned that an own proxy of <= 450 s in winter means a long taxi (bin mean 1,611-1,647 s, prediction 1,641-1,718) but cannot separate the 25-minute from the 40-minute flight inside that bin (p50 1,555, p90 2,394) and hedges the 10-20 min flights up to pay for it. (b) At LTFM the model saw the weather (`wx_deice` on 63-66 % of rows, 2-4 cm of 24-h snowfall, 8 cm depth) and lifted every row to 2,000-3,000 s instead of forming a regime. (c) At LIRF, LEMD and LEBL the held aircraft (block set, waiting at the stand) are invisible to the proxy and the arrivals, so the uplift is smeared over the bulk. Thin evidence: the EGLL dry-frost class is two days and its fog-at-or-below-0 C class three; EDDF has one real snow day; LSZH's > 60 min band is seven rows; EDDM's 24 and 31 Dec run holiday schedules whose departure ratios (0.66) overstate cancellations; the January numbers at every airport come from the v44 blend with the ADS-B stage and are not readings of the feature model alone.

## 6. Recommendations: concrete, testable encodings

Each item names the quantity the 2025 data measured, the airports it applies to and where to read the result. Winter features are judged on `exp029_winter_check.yaml` / `exp040` (Feb+Dec 2025: `rmse_disrupted`, `rmse_ex_dayoffset`), never on Jan+Jul 2025, which has no snow at LTFM, EDDM's December week or LFPG's November spell; bulk changes read `rmse_bulk` (±0.15 s floor). The most informative single test is still pending: **the base model on the November 2025 days (EHAM 20 / 23 / 25 Nov, LFPG 21-23 Nov, LSZH 21 Nov, EDDF 22-23 Nov), the closest twins of 2026, which are in neither OOF** (a config with a November holdout).

1. **Hour-of-event features: hours since the first and since the last METAR snow / freezing / LVP report of the day, and the trailing 1-3 h counts, interacted with the local hour.** Scale: the uplift reaches its peak 1-2 h after onset (LTFM 19 Feb: +160 one hour after the first snow report, +2,181 after two, +4,000 after three; LFPG 23 Nov snow 02-07Z, peak 07-08 local +1,430 / +1,700; EDDM 5 Jan snow 00-04Z, peak 08 local +2,846) and is back within ±60 s two to four hours after the last report (EHAM 5 Jan 13 local +52 after snow ended ~11; EDDF 17 Dec gone by 16 after LVP to 14Z; EGLL 31 Dec zero by 14 after FZFG to 11:20Z); it rebuilds if precipitation restarts (EDDM 30 Dec second wave 16-18 +977..+1,105). `era5_snowfall_3h` exists; METAR-based `snow_h_last3`, `frz_h_last3`, `lvp_h_last3`, `h_since_first_wx`, `h_since_last_wx` are the additions. Expected: the 1-2 h lag at each surge (-150..-850 s at the peak hour at LFPG, -348 at EGLL 31 Dec 10 h, -2,950 at LTFM) and the +150..+250 s flank overshoot shrink.
2. **Bank position x de-icing state x airport**, not a daily flag. Morning bank 05-10 local: EHAM +180..+400 per departure (freezing fog twice that), LFPG snow +300..+840, EDDM +590..+890 to 13 local, LEMD frost +280..+360 at 06-08 then zero, EGLL snow +340 at 08-09, LSZH +100..+500; midday normal except under persistent LVP (LFPG +760 / +850 at 11-12); evening bank 18-23 only under persisting / returning weather (LFPG FZFG +250..+640, LSZH +66..+362 with the 23 h curfew hour at +360..+400 and 18-20 % above 30 min, EDDM when snow continues +300..+1,006). Overnight 00-05 at LFPG +500..+670 (15-25 departures) and at LTFM 03-06 +1,300..+1,460.
3. **Per-airport correction of proxy-anchored features under de-icing / LVP state.** Multiply the live proxy-anomaly features (or add an airport x `wx_deice` x proxy interaction) by 1.5 (+112 s) at LTFM, 1.41 at LSZH, ~1.3 at EDDM (OLS 1.27), 1.04 at EDDF, 1.0 at EHAM (no correction), **0.63-0.71 at EGLL**; at LFPG add a separate "LVP / freezing-fog gap" term of +150..+300 s per departure (+530..+850 in the 05-12 wave) that is zero under dry frost; at LIRF / LEMD / LEBL carry the proxy with ~0 weight on event hours (slopes 0.46 / 0.08 / -0.03) and use items 5 and 6 instead. Test: the band bias on 30-60 min rows and the hourly bias on 17 Jan / 5 Dec (LFPG), 4 Jan evening (LSZH), 19 / 23 Feb (LTFM), and the +57 / +41 over-prediction on EGLL's 4 and 1 Jan.
